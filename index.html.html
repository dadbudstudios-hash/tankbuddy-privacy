TankBuddy Privacy Policy

Developer: DadBud Studios · Last updated: October 29, 2026

TankBuddy is an aquarium-keeping helper app. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: your tanks stay on your phone, there is no TankBuddy account, and we do not sell your data.

Information stored on your phone

Your tanks, fish, water test results, plants, reminders, streak, the name you type in, your language and sound settings are saved only on your device. DadBud Studios does not receive or store them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

If you use Backup & restore, the app creates a text backup that you choose where to share (for example Google Drive, email or a notes app). That copy is then handled by the service you chose.

Tank photos and camera

If you add a photo to a tank, the app uses your camera or photo gallery only when you choose to. The photo is copied into the app's own storage on your phone and is never uploaded to us.

Notifications

Feeding and water-change reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. No reminder information is sent to us.

Species photos

Fish photos are loaded from Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons. When a photo loads, your device connects to Wikimedia's servers, which receive standard technical information such as your IP address. See the Wikimedia privacy policy.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and app usage to show and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent first. You can change your ad consent at any time in Me → Ad privacy choices (shown where required), and reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

TankBuddy Pro is bought through Google Play. Payment is handled by Google; we never see your card details. To confirm and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-checking service. RevenueCat receives your purchase details from Google Play (such as the product, date and purchase token) together with a random ID created by the app. It does not receive your name or email from TankBuddy. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Sentry, a crash-reporting service, so we can fix the problem. A report contains technical details such as the error, the screen, the app version, phone model and Android version. It does not include your tanks, your name or other things you type, and we turned off sending IP addresses and personal data. Reports are kept for a limited time and used only to fix bugs. See the Sentry privacy policy.

Children

TankBuddy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Delete your data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone settings.
  • Buy Pro to remove ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
